Archival Note #01

Phosphor Decay & Scanlines

Early CRT monitors exhibited a unique temporal persistence. Phosphor glow lingering for milliseconds softened sharp pixel boundaries and created an organic warmth lost in high-DPI panels.

Archival Note #02

The Programmable Sound Generator

From the General Instrument AY-3-8910 to Commodore’s SID 6581, early game audio was pure mathematics: square wave oscillators, noise registers, and envelope generators creating enduring melody.

Archival Note #03

Uncompromising Mechanics

Born from coin-operated economics, early video games expected precision. There were no mid-level checkpoints, no tutorials—only the player's reflexes and spatial intuition against pure algorithmic logic.
